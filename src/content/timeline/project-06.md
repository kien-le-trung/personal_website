summary: "Analyzed longitudinal patient encounters from Stormont Vail Health to model complete patient journeys rather than isolated visits. Our team used a Cox proportional hazards model to predict when a patient's next encounter would occur and multinomial logistic regression to predict the next department, achieving 70% test performance for the Cox model and 40% validation accuracy for the department model against a 5% baseline. The project also reinforced that communicating results clearly is as important as the modeling itself."
